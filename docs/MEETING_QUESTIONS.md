# Questions for AutoHDR

Pass 1. The users doc is provisional until the starred items are answered. Everything here is something the candidate could not answer firsthand. Claims in `USERS.md` that come only from the PRD are the ones these questions resolve.

## Load-bearing (the answer changes who the user is or which moments we target)
- ★ What does a novice photographer do today when an agent asks for a video: decline, outsource, use a template tool, or skip it? — unblocks: Sections 2, 3, UC-1 "why this shape".
- ★ When you compare the original and a reproduction, what makes it "nearly identical" and what makes it fail: the music, the shot movements, the cuts, or the look of the shots? — unblocks: Section 6 (F5 acceptance).
- ★ Must creators be able to author styles through the product, and by which method (demo shoot, past-video upload, storyboard outline), or is a hand-built style for the demo enough? — unblocks: UC-2, Section 6 (F4).
- ★ Who judges the result, and how: AutoHDR reviewers watching the side by side, or something else? — unblocks: F5, F9 acceptance.
