# S16 - The Jobs list and the Job page show progress, failures and the finished video

**Kind:** module (M) | **Blocked by:** S01 | **Review gate:** agents
**Status:** landed by agents (no human demo). Story gate green after the review fixes: 560 passed, 5 skipped.

## Summary

The agent can see what every job is doing without asking anyone. S16 builds two server-rendered screens. The Jobs screen lists every job, newest first, with its property, style, kind, submit time, elapsed time and a status chip. The Job screen shows one job in each state: running (which shot is on which attempt, refreshed every 5 s without a reload), waiting behind another job, stopped with the reason and "Start again", and ready, with failed shots listed beside the player. It also shows each shot's crop-only and fallback-model notes, a "Redo this shot" button, the player, the download link and the song credit. Both pages read through `queue.list_jobs`, `queue.get_job` and `queue.get_video`, which the tests stub; S22 and S23 wire them to real jobs.

## What was built

- `app/web/templates/jobs.html`, `app/web/pages/jobs.py`. The Jobs screen: one row per job from `queue.list_jobs` (already newest first), with Property, Style, Kind, Submitted (local time), Elapsed, a status chip and "Open".
- `app/web/templates/job.html`, `app/web/pages/job.py`. The Job screen.
  - While queued or running, `#job` polls `GET /jobs/{id}` every 5 s and swaps itself in place (`hx-select="#job"`, `outerHTML`). The finished page has no `hx-trigger`, so polling stops on its own.
  - One banner per state: info "Making your video: N of M shots finished", warn "Waiting for job a1b2c3d4 to finish" (linked), error with the stop reason and "Start again", ok "Your video is ready / Took ...".
  - Player card: the failed or skipped shots each with their reason and the check's notes, the `<video>`, "Download video", version and size, and the song credit with its source link.
  - Shot cards: chip, "move · speed · length · transition", reason, status detail, "Photo edit not used (crop only): ...", "Made with the fallback model (...)", "Redo this shot".
- `app/web/jobs_view.py`. Display helpers: chip labels and tones, stop messages, failure reasons, `credit_parts`, `elapsed`, `words`.
- `app/web/static/jobs.js`. Shows `<time>` values in the browser's local time, also after each swap. Handles the "Start again" and "Redo" answers: reload on success, the route's `{error, message}` in place on failure, and a short message when a poll fails.
- `app/web/static/jobs.css`. Screen, banner, chip, card and player styles, linked only from the two S16 templates.
- `app/shared/types.py`, `app/jobs/reading.py`. `JobVideo.version`, read from `jobs.video_version`. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` Shared types and the contract test's `VIDEO_KEYS` were updated to match.
- `app/jobs/queue.py`, `app/jobs/stubs.py`. `get_video` answers the stub job with stub links, like `get_job` already did.
- `tests/support/job_screens.py` (synthetic jobs and the `stub_jobs` fixture), `tests/e2e/test_jobs_page.py`, `tests/e2e/test_job_page.py` (Playwright, Chromium).

## Key decisions and assumptions

- **The chip says "Passed", not the wireframe's "Done".** Criterion 4 says the shot "shows passed", and the story outranks the wireframe. "Trying again" (the wireframe's chip) shows while a shot is on attempt 2 or later.
- **Shots are cards, not a table.** The story says "shot table"; the wireframe draws cards with exactly the per-shot content the story lists, so the wireframe's layout was used.
- **`version` was added to `Job.video`** (INDEX architectural issue 3, named in the plan), so the page can show "Version 2" and name the download `video-<id>-v2.mp4`.
- **`queue.get_video` returns stub links for the stub job**, mirroring the stub branch in `get_job`, so the stub job's page still renders. S22 removes both.
- **Nav bar, page background and font were left to S15.** S15 owns `app.css` and `base.html` and was building in parallel, so S16's styles and script live in new `jobs.css` and `jobs.js` files linked only from its own templates.
- "Beside the player" (INDEX issue 1) is read as the list of failed and skipped shots at the top of the player card; each failed shot card is also marked.
- "Job A" in criterion 5 is shown as the running job's short id, linked to its page [assumed].
- Test videos are VP8 WebM generated by ffmpeg into `tmp_path`, because Playwright's Chromium cannot decode H.264. Nothing is committed (GC-9). The download test uses a small local HTTP server, because Chromium downloads bypass Playwright's request routing.
- PRD vs docs: no disagreement found for this story.
