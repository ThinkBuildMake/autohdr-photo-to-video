## Summary

S13 builds the Comparison module: `recordCuts` measures where each finished video's cuts landed against the job's own plan and stores one `cut_checks` row per cut. `POST /api/styles/{id}/reference` marks a ready job made from the style's text as the style's reference video. `GET /api/styles/{id}/reproductions/{jobId}` lays a reproduction beside that reference: both video links, both summaries, one row per shot position with cut errors and flags, the song and the text.

## What was built

- `app/comparison/service.py`.
  - `record_cuts(job_id)`: reads the job's video key and shots, keeps the shots in the video (not removed, not failed or skipped), and computes each expected cut as the running sum of earlier in-video shot lengths. The row's position is the incoming shot's. Detected cuts are matched to expected cuts nearest pair first, each detected cut used once, within 0.5 s. Delete and insert share one transaction, so a re-check replaces the rows. If the detector raises, every row gets `detected_s` null, a warning names the job and the exception type, and the job is never touched.
  - `set_reference(style_id, job_id)`: 404 `style_not_found`, 404 `job_not_found`, then 409 `not_eligible` unless the job is `ready` and its text equals the style's. The check and the write run in one `session_scope`; the write goes through `library.set_reference_job(..., session)`.
  - `get_reproduction(style_id, job_id)`: errors in order 404 style, 404 job, 409 `no_reference`, 409 `not_ready` (either job not `ready`/`ready_with_failures`, or without a video). Summaries count in-video shots: shot count, total and median length, movements, and transitions on shots after the first. Rows are the union of both jobs' positions. A reproduction shot is flagged when it is out of the video, used the fallback model, or was edited. `maxCutErrorS` is the largest |error| over both videos. `song.credit` is the reproduction's song.
- `app/comparison/stubs.py`: now only `detect_cuts` (Files.get + Media.detectCuts, removed by S22) and `presign_download` (Files.presignDownload, removed by S25). `stub_reproduction` and the "Stub until S13" leftovers are deleted; Comparison no longer calls `queue.get_job`.
- `app/library/service.py`: `set_reference_job(style_id, job_id, session=None)`. Without a session it behaves as before (FK error → 404 `job_not_found`).
- Tests: `tests/comparison/test_record_cuts.py` (new), `tests/contract/test_comparison_routes.py` (rewritten), `tests/support/comparison_rows.py` (new raw-SQL helpers).
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`: `cut_checks.detected_s` now states the 0.5 s matching window.

## Key decisions and assumptions

- **0.5 s matching window.** A detected cut counts for an expected cut only within 0.5 s; further off it is stored as not found. This is consistent with VR-2: its ±0.1 s is the pass threshold the recorded error is judged against, not a matching rule. A window of ±0.1 s would turn every out-of-limit cut into a null and lose the error that VR-2, EV-1 and EV-2 report. The VR-2 guard test keeps a 0.3 s error. Written into ARCHITECTURE at `cut_checks.detected_s`.
- **Each detected cut is used once, nearest pair first.** Without it, a short shot (3.0, 0.4, 3.0 s with one cut found at 3.0) recorded a missed cut as a −0.4 s error.
- **setReferenceJob is called for real, in one transaction.** The story listed Library.setReferenceJob as stubbed until S25. The build calls the real function with the caller's session, so the eligibility check and the write cannot be split by another writer, and Comparison never writes `styles` (the ownership gate passes). S25 has nothing to unstub there.
- **Eligible reference** = status exactly `ready` and the style's text unchanged (ARCHITECTURE Must hold). `ready_with_failures` is refused. A reproduction may be `ready_with_failures`; its failed shots show as flagged.
- The detector warning logs only the exception type and the job id, never the message, so no key or link can reach the logs.
- PRD vs docs: no conflict found; no doc was overridden.
