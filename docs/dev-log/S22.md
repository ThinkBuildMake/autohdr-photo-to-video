# S22 - Make a real video: the worker generates every shot and the photographer plays and downloads it

**Kind:** wiring | **Blocked by:** S05, S07, S10, S12, S13, S16, S21, S28, S29, S30 | **Review gate:** human demo

## Summary

A submitted job now runs through the real Jobs, Pipeline, Providers, Media, Files and Comparison modules instead of stand-ins. The worker edits each photo, makes and checks each clip, assembles the video, records the cuts and writes the job, shot and attempt records. The Job page follows the job from waiting to a playable, downloadable video. The gate runs the whole path with mocked OpenRouter answers, moto for the bucket and the real media tools.

What is not proven: AC1 (a playable video within 30 minutes of submitting, on the deployed app with real providers) and the real visual quality of edited photos and clips. The gate test checks that the seams join and that the stored numbers agree. It is not timing evidence. The human demo needs paid provider calls and has not been started.

## What was built

1. **Photo and provider interfaces.** Photoshoots returns the original bytes of one photo for the worker. `edit_photo` takes an optional explicit model. With a model given, only that model is tried. Without it, the old per-prompt fallback is unchanged.
2. **Worker lifecycle.** `runner.run_next_job` now runs the whole shot lifecycle: photo edit, clip, check, retries, fallback model, assembly. Jobs stores the current round and the earlier attempts, so Pipeline never reads Jobs tables. The in-memory bucket and the Pipeline stubs are gone. A failed shot is stored as failed with its reason instead of being swallowed. A fallback model recomputes the clip length. Each clip has at most four attempts per round.
3. **Cut detection.** `Comparison.recordCuts` downloads the video from Files and runs the real Media detector. A missing or unreadable video still leaves a null record. The reproduction signing stub stays for S25.
4. **Full-job gate test.** One test submits a job and runs it through every real seam. It has a flagged then passed shot, a failed shot, and three surviving shots with contrasting footage. It reads the metrics report and compares it with values worked out independently.
5. **Job page.** Close and reopen, a second job waiting behind the first, the song credit, only the passed clip, the edited or crop-only result per shot, playback, and a download whose length matches. The Job read model gained `photoCount` and `waitingBehindLabel`.

## Decisions

- **Timing clock (PF-1).** The PRD gives no clock. S19's distinction stands: PF-1 is submit to the first playable finish, so queue wait counts and a queued job can miss it. The worker's own budget runs from claim plus 30 minutes. The deployed demo must measure submit to playable at 1800 s or less and report a miss honestly. No hard completion guarantee is claimed, because a provider clip timeout is longer than the two-minute reserve before admission.
- **Explicit edit model.** An explicit model means only that model. The hidden fallback stays only for callers that pass none, so Pipeline decides the fallback order.
- **Shared cancellation for photo edits.** The photo chain shares the job's stop flag with the clip attempts and checks it before every edit, retry, fallback and edit check.
- **Distinguishable media in the full-seam test.** Flagged, passed and failed clips have different footage, and the test decodes frames of the finished video. A pointer check alone passed even when the flagged clip was assembled.
- **Wireframe on the Job page.** The Job page was brought to the wireframe: photo label and count in the header, the original and edited first frame side by side, the partly-failed banner above the player, a ready line with the time taken, and the attempt count read from the current round's stored attempts.
- **Job read model.** `photoCount` and `waitingBehindLabel` were added (INDEX architectural issue 11).
- **Media wiring.** Stills are probed, the video is 1920 by 1080 at the style's 24 or 30 fps, and the test checks the assembled object. Cost is the sum of provider calls.

## Bugs found by review

- **Edits kept spending after a stop (subtask 2).** When one shot stopped the job for lack of credit, another shot's photo chain went on with edits and checks. Fixed with the shared cancellation above and a concurrent-stop test.
- **The full-job test could not tell flagged from passed (subtask 4).** The clips had identical bytes, so assembling the flagged attempt still passed. Two scratch mutations showed it. Fixed with distinguishable footage and decoded-frame checks.
- **Only one cut was checked (subtask 4).** With two surviving shots, using each shot's length instead of cumulative boundaries still passed. Fixed with three surviving shots and every cumulative boundary asserted.
- **Job page missed the wireframe (subtask 5).** No photo label, no paired previews, the banner inside the player card. Fixed in round 2. The orchestrator's own screenshot check found three more: no label and count in the header, "1 of 4" beside "after 4 attempts", and no ready line. Fixed in round 3.
- **F18 interaction.** F18 merged the provider-call module while S22 was open. After the rebase the media wiring test imported the old module. The import was updated and the gate ran green at 824 passed, 5 skipped.
