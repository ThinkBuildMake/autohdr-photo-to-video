# S15 - The Styles page and the New video page: pick a style, upload photos, check the shot plan

**Kind:** module (M) | **Blocked by:** S01 | **Review gate:** agents
**Status:** built by agents and reviewed once, with the round 1 fixes applied. Story gate after the fixes: 567 passed, 5 skipped.

## Summary

This is where the photographer starts. The Styles page shows each preset's name, author, text and song. A style with a reference video gets a mark, a "See reproductions" link and a "Make a reproduction" picker, which starts a job on another shoot with the style's text unchanged.

The New video page has two steps:
- **Style and photos.** The style text is filled from a preset and can be edited. The song picker has a preview button, and "Save as my style" saves the text. The drop zone uploads photos straight to the bucket. Refused files are listed with their reasons, and the photo grid shows each photo's room and subject, or says the rooms are not labelled.
- **Check the shot plan.** The page polls the plan until it is ready. It then shows one editable card per shot (photo, movement, speed, length, transition, photo edits, plan note), with the planner's notes in a banner above the cards. Shots can be changed, added and removed. A failed plan shows its reason and a "Try again" button. "Make my video" saves the plan, submits it and opens the Job page.

The screens follow the wireframe's layout, wording, colours and type. Both pages call the real `/api/...` routes, and the browser tests answer those routes with Playwright stand-ins.

## Key decisions and assumptions

- **Stubbed routes** means Playwright `page.route` answers the API in the browser tests. The pages call the real routes. New video and the reproduction control each use a small plain JavaScript file: uploads go from the browser to the bucket and the plan is JSON, which HTMX forms do not fit. HTMX stays loaded.
- **`listPhotoshoots`** was added to the ARCHITECTURE Photoshoots interface and recorded as resolved issue 9 in `docs/stories/INDEX.md`. The shoot pickers need it: S15 now, S18 and S25 later.
- Upload tickets are paired with the accepted files by position. The page drops refused files by createUpload's own rule (JPG or PNG, at most 25 MB) and keeps the drop order.
- The page calls completeUpload even when a bucket post fails, so a photo that did not reach the bucket is listed "did not arrive".
- "Make a reproduction" and "See reproductions" only appear on styles that have a reference video.
- "Save as my style" sends `authorName: "you"`.
- "Make my video" saves the plan before submitting. "Next" and "Make my video" are disabled while their request runs.
- An edited or added shot gets `planNote` `changed_by_photographer`, shown as "Changed by you".
- The song line shows "title by artist, CC licence", never the source link, as the wireframe does. The full credit with its link stays on the video (GC-5).
- The page polls labels and the plan every 2 s.
- No doc disagreed with the PRD.
