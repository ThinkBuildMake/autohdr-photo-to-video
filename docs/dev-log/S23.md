## Summary

A stopped or failed job now says why in plain words, "Start again" re-makes only the unfinished shots, "Redo this shot" re-makes one shot, and a worker restart is recovered on its own. The gate runs these paths through the real Jobs, Pipeline, Providers, Files, Media and Comparison modules with mocked OpenRouter answers, moto for the bucket and a real browser for the Job page. Out of credit, rate limit and refusal each have a case.

What is not proven: the six human demo steps. Steps 1 to 4 need a local instance with `FAKE_PROVIDERS=1` and `scripts/demo_s23.py`; steps 5 and 6 need the deployed app with a real job. The gate shows that the seams join and that the stored numbers agree. It is not evidence about real providers, a real container restart or real timing.

## What was built

1. **Restart and redo invariants.** Putting a job back in the queue clears the run's start, deadline and finish times, so a restarted job gets a fresh 30-minute budget and a fresh clock. "Redo this shot" is refused with 409 `pending_edits` while the photographer has unapplied edits, so a redo promises exactly one shot. Each shot keeps its history: earlier rounds and attempts stay as they were, a redo opens a new round, and the four-attempt limit applies per round. ARCHITECTURE and the story index were updated to match (the pending-edits rule, the cleared times).
2. **Startup recovery across real modules.** One test file starts the worker and the app over real Jobs, Pipeline and Providers. A job that was running becomes stopped with "the worker restarted", finished and queued work is left alone, an open clip call at the provider has its clip downloaded before the call is closed, and a shoot that was labelling and a job that was planning both resume without the photographer doing anything. Only test files were added.
3. **Visible failures and per-error metrics.** `jobs/reading.py` gives a retried shot a "previous attempt" line, and the Job page wording says "flagged every attempt" and "the worker restarted" with the Start again button. Metrics panel 3 now has a row per kind of error with its time to the page. The fake provider can be told to answer out of credit, rate limit, refuse or flag a shot, and to clear it again. `scripts/demo_s23.py` drives those scenarios for the local demo. A shared `s23_world` fixture runs the failure scenarios (402, 429, refusal, flags) through the real worker and checks each one is on the page in under a minute.
4. **Full-gate restart, redo and recovery.** An HTTP-level test submits a job, stops it on credit, restarts it and redoes shot 2. It compares attempt ids, rounds, bucket keys and stored bytes before and after, counts generation requests per shot, and checks that the version goes up by exactly one. Five browser cases use real persisted Jobs state with no stubbed job responses: credit stop to restart to ready to redo, pending edits rejected in the action error slot, flags and rate limits polled while another shot is in flight, partial delivery with the failed reason beside the player, and worker recovery. The browser serves a VP8 copy of the assembled video because the gate Chromium cannot decode H.264. The download stays the original MP4 and is compared byte for byte.
5. **Story review fixes.** See Bugs.

## Decisions

- **Job-stop time is not a fallback clock.** An edit error with no clip attempt used to take the job's stop time as its time to the page. The metrics now measure such an error only when it is the one that caused the stop (out of credit on a job stopped for credit). Any other attempt-less error is unmeasured, not guessed.
- **A paid clip is not thrown away on a bucket error.** Upload of a clip that already passed its check is retried on storage errors. If it still fails, the attempt is kept as an error with its cost and the shot goes on to its next attempt.
- **A crash at the finish does not kill the worker.** An exception from `finish_run` stops the job (assembly failed) and the worker keeps polling.
- **Stopped-job cost is summed on read.** The total comes from the provider calls when the job is read, so calls that finish after the stop are counted.
- **Recovered clips are reused.** A clip collected at startup is attached to its unfinished shot, so "Start again" does not pay for it twice.
- **Non-provider errors get their own reason.** A new failure reason `internal_error` has truthful wording instead of "No attempt succeeded". ARCHITECTURE's FailureReason list was updated.
- **Subtask 3 owns `reading.py`.** The RL-1 failure-reading fix lived there, outside the plan's file list. The orchestrator amended the plan after spec review found the ownership gap.
- **Editor-only mutation gaps stay with S24.** No UI files changed in this story.

## Bugs found by review

- **Early errors reported as hours to the page (story logic review, blocking).** The query fell back to the job's stop time for calls with no attempt. A rate limit in minute 1 of a job that stopped at minute 20 showed 19 minutes, and panel 3 broke AC10. Fixed as above, with a test of an attempt-less rate limit followed by a later stop.
- **This log described subtask 4 only (story spec review, blocking).** It said "no production changes", "uncommitted" and 748 passed with one failure. Rewritten for the whole story.
- **Paid clip lost on a bucket error.** One failed upload failed the shot and skipped the remaining attempts. Fixed with the retry above.
- **A crash at the finish killed the worker.** The job stayed running, and the next start would stop it and queue it again. Fixed with the stop above.
- **Stopped job showed too low a cost.** Fixed by summing on read.
- **Recovered clip never attached.** AC4 was met but the clip sat unused. Fixed by reuse on restart.
- **Wording test out of date (subtask 3).** The e2e check expected "Failed the check on every attempt"; the story and the new wording say "flagged every attempt". The test was changed to match, and a worker-restart banner case was added.
- **Sandbox failure (subtask 4).** `test_setup_steps_start_the_app` failed in the agent sandbox because uv could not create its cache lock. It passes in the gate. Nothing was changed to hide it.

## Open

- **The real deployed demo has not been run.** Steps 1 to 6 of the story are unproven on real providers and a real container restart. The demo needs paid providers and is waiting on the user's go-ahead.
- **Known edges (minors accepted for this story):**
  - A redo after an interrupted shot could reuse an old recovered clip.
  - A failed redo drops the shot's previous clip, so the new cut is ready with failures and lacks a shot that was fine before. A stopped redo leaves no clip for that shot until Start again.
  - The "Previous attempt" text can repeat the status line, or name an earlier error when a later attempt was flagged.
  - Merged with S24: `redo_shot` now uses the editor's shared `_awaiting_export` check, run first after the finished check, so there is one pending-edits rule for redo and export.
- **Carried from S22 and left:** photo edits only see the in-process stop flag, so a Stop from outside keeps spending on edits until the first Jobs write.
