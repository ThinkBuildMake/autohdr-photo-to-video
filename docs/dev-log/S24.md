## What was built

1. **Reliable editor mutations and pending-change bookkeeping.** A shot's pending change (new clip, re-cut only, removed) is tracked in Jobs and shown on its card. Redo on a shot with unexported edits answers 409 `pending_edits` instead of silently losing them. Editing a shot that has no photo answers 400 `invalid_edit`. A saved edit survives a reload. Removing and putting back a shot before re-exporting keeps its old clip and spends nothing.
2. **New video plan: Back, re-plan, cut-at times.** On the plan step, Back returns to the form with the style text, song and photos kept. Changing the style text and re-planning reuses the draft through `POST /api/jobs/{id}/plan`, with no new photoshoot or job. Each shot card shows its cut-at time, which follows lengths and removals. Submit stays disabled until navigation finishes, and a failed submit restores the controls and shows the error. A pending-navigation test that hung in the gate was rewritten to inspect the disabled controls directly.
3. **Real editor screen.** The page shows the timeline, a preview, and the paired original photo and edited first frame for the selected shot. It lists the 12 movements, speed, length and transition, the movement prompt and photo edit text, swap room and remove. It offers editing only on a ready video and shows a "being made" or "Re-exporting" banner with disabled controls when the job is busy. After a photo swap the old first frame is replaced by a placeholder until the re-export, so the pair cannot mismatch. Non-uploaded photos are filtered out of the swap list.
4. **Selective re-export through the real worker.** Provider calls are only for shots that need a new clip. Untouched shots keep the same rows, attempts and clip hashes. Changed shots get new final attempts, and a fast shot is remade fast. The assembler cuts the video as the next version, and every cut is read back and checked.

## Decisions

- **Redo with pending edits is refused, not merged.** A 409 `pending_edits` keeps the photographer's unexported edits intact. Redo and export stay separate actions.
- **Photo-less shots reject edits.** A shot with no uploaded photo cannot be given a movement or length, because the change could never be made.
- **Stored clip inputs decide "new clip" (story-review fix).** `needs_new_clip` was sticky: change a move to Crane Up and back, and export still paid for an identical clip. `job_shots` gained a nullable `clip_inputs` column holding what the clip was made from. An edit that returns to those inputs clears the flag. Recorded in ARCHITECTURE and INDEX architectural issue 12.
- **The last kept room cannot be removed (story-review fix).** Both the edit and the export refuse a result with zero kept shots, and the editor disables the last Remove button.
- **FR-1.6 behaviour kept when a changed shot's new clip fails.** The old clip is not kept as a fallback. The shot shows as failed in the new version, and the previous video version is still in the bucket. Keeping the old clip until the new one passes was considered and not built, so a failure stays visible and the redo rules from S23 apply.
- **The old version is never replaced.** Each re-export writes a new video version and the previous key stays readable.
- **Mocked time is not evidence.** The 10 minute check runs click to decoded download on mocks. Real timing is demo evidence only.

## Bugs found by review

- **Removing every shot locked the editor (story review).** Remove all shots, click Re-export: export accepted it, queued nothing, the worker stopped the job with no shot passed, and the stopped job refused all edits and restarts. One click locked a finished video for good. Fixed with the last-room rule above, a backstop in export, a disabled button, and tests at each layer.
- **Undo still remade the clip (story review).** See the stored clip inputs decision. Fixed with `clip_inputs` and tests that move a value and put it back.
- **Dev log scoped to one subtask (story spec review).** The first draft covered only subtask 4. This is the rewrite.
- **Plan navigation test hung in the gate (subtask 2).** It waited on a pending navigation while the controls it asserted on were disabled. Fixed by reading the disabled state directly and releasing the navigation in a finally block, with every assertion kept.
- **Browser playback test used an unavailable audio encoder (subtask 3).** The fixture asked for an encoder the environment lacks. Switched to VP8 with Opus, nothing else changed.
- **Old first frame after a swap (subtask 3 review).** The new photo sat next to the old photo's frame under an "Edited first frame" caption. Fixed with the placeholder above, along with the missing Re-exporting banner.

## Open

- **The real deployed demo has not been run.** AC3 real timing and the look of the re-cut video are unproven. The demo needs paid providers and is waiting on the user's go-ahead.
- **Minors from the reviews:**
  - The plan step's cut-at time is a plain running sum and ignores the crossfade overlap, so a crossfade shot's real cut is earlier by its transition length.
  - Re-uploading after Back drops the draft and creates a new one, so the old draft is orphaned.
  - Swapping a photo keeps the old room's photo-edit prompts, so the new photo gets the old edit chain on re-export.
  - A re-upload's Start-from choice is ignored when the video is re-planned.
  - S23 also edits the redo and restart rules in `app/jobs/edits.py`. The `pending_edits` guard may conflict with it on merge. Re-run the Jobs edit tests after merging.
